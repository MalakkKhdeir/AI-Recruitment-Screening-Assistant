Job Title: Technical Project Manager

We are looking for a Technical Project Manager to lead and coordinate technical projects from planning to completion.

Responsibilities:
- Plan, organize, and manage technical projects.
- Define project scope, goals, deliverables, and timelines.
- Coordinate between technical teams, developers, engineers, and stakeholders.
- Track project progress, deadlines, and deliverables.
- Identify project risks and help develop mitigation plans.
- Communicate project status and updates to stakeholders.
- Ensure projects are delivered on time and according to requirements.
- Manage project documentation and maintain clear project records.
- Support teams in solving technical and project-related issues.

Required Skills:
- Project management
- Technical project management
- Communication and leadership
- Team coordination
- Problem solving
- Time management
- Risk management
- Agile and Scrum methodologies
- Understanding of software development life cycle (SDLC)
- Basic understanding of software and technology concepts
- Microsoft Excel and project management tools

Education:
- Bachelor's degree or diploma in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, Information Technology, AI, Robotics Engineering, or a related technical field.

Experience:
- Experience managing technical or software projects is preferred.
- Experience working with cross-functional teams is a plus.
- Academic or personal technical projects are acceptable for entry-level candidates.

Preferred:
- Experience with Jira, Trello, or similar project management tools.
- Knowledge of Git and GitHub.
- Familiarity with APIs, databases, cloud services, and software development workflows.
- Experience with Agile/Scrum teams.
- Strong documentation and reporting skills.

Languages:
- English
- Arabic
